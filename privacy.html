Privacy Policy

Vyxbox Assistant · Last updated 10 November 2026

What this app is

Vyxbox Assistant is a private, self-hosted tool used only by the owner of the Vyxbox YouTube channel to manage that channel. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Google user data it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (granted through Google sign-in), the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • read the channel's videos, playlists, captions and comments;
  • upload videos, thumbnails and captions, and edit video details and playlists;
  • post, edit and delete the channel's own comments and replies, and moderate comments on the channel's videos.

Public comments read from the channel include the commenter's public display name and comment text, as YouTube shows them on the video page.

How data is stored and used

  • Everything runs on the channel owner's own computer. There is no Vyxbox server and no database.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored locally on that computer and is used only to call the YouTube Data API.
  • Comment text may be kept in local report files to help the owner decide which comments to reply to. To draft reply suggestions, comment text may be sent to an AI model provider chosen by the owner; it is used only to produce that suggestion.
  • Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Limited Use

Vyxbox Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube data is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's local folder removes all data it stored.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised through the Vyxbox YouTube channel.
